LABS — COMPANY POLICIES

1. REFUND POLICY
Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of purchase. The item must be unused and proof of purchase is required. Refund requests are reviewed by the support team. A refund is not guaranteed until approved.

2. CUSTOMER SUPPORT HOURS
The customer support team operates Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The team is closed on weekends and public holidays.

3. ESCALATION POLICY
If an employee cannot answer a customer question using approved documentation, they must escalate the case to a human support specialist. Employees must not invent policies or promise outcomes that have not been approved.

4. CANCELLATION POLICY
Customers may request order cancellation before the order has been dispatched. Once an order has been dispatched, the customer must contact support to discuss available options. Cancellation is not guaranteed until confirmed by the support team.
